## What are population balances?

Population balance equations describe systems containing entities distributed according to one or more internal properties, such as particle size, composition, age or morphology.

They are widely used to model processes involving:

* crystallization;
* precipitation;
* aerosols and nanoparticles;
* droplets and bubbles;
* polymerization;
* granulation;
* biological populations;
* other particulate and multiphase systems.

## Learning objectives

The material will help students:

1. formulate population balance equations;
2. understand number-density functions and internal coordinates;
3. identify relevant growth, aggregation, breakage and nucleation mechanisms;
4. select appropriate numerical solution methods;
5. implement population-balance models;
6. interpret and validate simulation results.

## Main topics

### Fundamentals

* Population balance equations
* Number-density functions
* Internal and external coordinates
* Initial and boundary conditions

### Physical processes

* Growth and dissolution
* Nucleation
* Aggregation and coalescence
* Breakage and fragmentation

### Solution methods

* Discretization methods
* Method of moments
* Quadrature-based moment methods
* Monte Carlo methods

### Applications

* Crystallization and precipitation
* Aerosols and nanoparticles
* Droplets, bubbles and multiphase flows
